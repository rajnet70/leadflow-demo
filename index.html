🎯 Opportunity Score Methodology
How LeadFlow ranks accounts within a territory

LeadFlow combines multiple market, commercial, operational and digital signals into a single Opportunity Score to help sales teams prioritize accounts within a territory. Every account is evaluated across six dimensions and assigned a score from 0 to 100 — creating a defensible, data-driven priority order that removes guesswork from territory planning.
Market Presence
Visibility & Market Engagement
Measures the strength, visibility and engagement of an account within its local market. Accounts with strong market presence signal established operations and active customer relationships.
Commercial Potential
Revenue Opportunity & Growth Indicators
Identifies indicators associated with revenue opportunity and business growth. Accounts scoring highly here represent the strongest commercial targets within the territory.
Service Profile
Breadth & Complexity of Services
Evaluates the breadth and complexity of services offered. Accounts with broader, more complex service profiles signal larger operations and greater purchasing capacity.
Digital Maturity
Operational & Engagement Capabilities
Measures adoption of modern operational and customer engagement capabilities. Digitally mature accounts are more likely to invest in new solutions and technology partnerships.
Accessibility
Customer Discovery & Engagement
Evaluates how easily customers can discover and engage with the business. Accessible accounts signal active investment in patient acquisition and business development.
Territory Fit
Alignment with Territory Opportunity Patterns
Measures alignment with opportunity patterns observed across the territory. Accounts with strong territory fit closely match the profile of high-performing accounts in this market.
Score Tiers:   Tier 1 (70–100) — Highest opportunity accounts. Prioritize immediately.  ·  Tier 2 (40–69) — Strong secondary opportunities. Schedule follow-up.  ·  Tier 3 (0–39) — Emerging accounts. Monitor for growth signals.

💡 Strategic Territory Observations
OPPORTUNITY CLUSTER
Midtown Manhattan is the highest-density opportunity zone
The highest concentration of Tier 1 accounts sits within Midtown and Midtown East — driven by a combination of high review volumes, multi-service practices and strong technology adoption. Territory reps should prioritize this zone for initial outreach.
TECHNOLOGY ADOPTION
Digital imaging is the dominant technology signal — adoption is not uniform
58% of tech-enabled practices use digital imaging, but only 16% have patient portals and 28% offer teledentistry. This gap represents a significant upsell opportunity for technology providers targeting practices already on a digital adoption path.
SERVICE MIX INSIGHT
Implants + Cosmetic is the highest-value service combination in this territory
Practices offering both implants and cosmetic dentistry consistently score in Tier 1 — these practices signal high investment in equipment, premium patient profiles and revenue-generating procedures. They represent the strongest targets for premium product positioning.
DIGITAL MATURITY TREND
50% online booking adoption signals a market in active digital transition
Half of all analyzed practices have adopted online booking — but the other half have not. Practices without online booking are often in an earlier stage of digital adoption, representing an addressable market for patient experience and practice management solutions.
MARKET CONCENTRATION
Tier 1 accounts are geographically concentrated — enabling efficient territory coverage
The majority of Tier 1 Opportunity accounts are concentrated across Midtown, Financial District, Tribeca and the Upper East Side — allowing a territory rep to cover the highest-priority accounts efficiently within a focused geographic radius.
